# Support on-demand curated encounters with dynamic direction

Musement allows the user to actively pull a bounded set of fresh Discoveries on demand (`musement pull`), optionally providing a transient Curiosity Direction (such as a specific field or topic) and target count, rather than relying exclusively on daily pushed editions.

Daily push cadences create artificial attention debt and guilt when a user's daily bandwidth is constrained. Because Musement prioritizes timeless quality and intellectual novelty over transient news cycles, editorial ranking and selection are performed online at query time against the durable Candidate Pool. The dynamic Curiosity Direction guides the transient encounter without permanently altering the user's Interest Profile.
